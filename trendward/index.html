THE TRENDWARD APPROACH ●
01
Potential Energy

Catalysts, structure, float, liquidity conditions, and other reasons a stock may be capable of repricing.

Ignition

Abnormal volume, dollar-volume acceleration, relative liquidity, and changing market activity.

03
Price Confirmation

VWAP retention, resistance breaks, price progress, higher lows, and evidence that the market agrees.

WHY I BUILT IT

More than a stock screener.

Traditional screeners can produce long lists of stocks and isolated metrics. I wanted something that could help answer a more useful question:

What is changing right now, and is the setup becoming more interesting or less interesting?

Trendward combines market data, engineered features, machine learning, and rule-based market context to continuously narrow the field and surface the setups that deserve deeper attention.

HOW IT WORKS

From a large universe to a focused watchlist.

01

Discover

Trendward scans the eligible market for changes in price, liquidity, volume acceleration, dollar volume, and market structure.

02

Analyze

Candidates are evaluated using engineered point-in-time features, model probabilities, scanner context, and structural evidence.

03

Confirm

Price behavior matters. Trendward looks for constructive response around VWAP, highs, resistance, and continued price progress.

04

Monitor

Stronger candidates remain under observation so their setup phase, persistence, evidence, and weakening conditions can be followed over time.

THE PRODUCT

Intelligence designed around decisions, not data overload.

The interface is organized around a small number of questions: What is strongest? What am I watching? Why is it still worth watching? And how did the setup get here?

SCANNER

Find emerging setups

Focuses attention on the strongest candidates based on current liquidity, price action, model evidence, and scanner state.

WATCHLIST

Know why it still matters

Tracks active setups with scanner persistence, setup phase, supporting evidence, and the conditions that would weaken the thesis.

TIMELINE

See how the setup evolved

Replays real recorded scanner sessions so changes in liquidity, price structure, model evidence, and scanner state can be examined over time.

MODEL CONTEXT

Use ML as evidence

Machine-learning probabilities supplement the broader market context rather than replacing price action or market structure.

UNDER THE HOOD
